# 🎯 SGP / Parlay Rules

## SGP construction
- ML + correlated K prop = same game, pitcher dominates = correlated ✅
- ML + hit prop same team = correlated ✅
- ML -150+ = SGP only, never standalone
- Target combined odds +150 to +300 for SGP
- Identify the correlated chain before building SGP — worked for PHI ([[2026-06-21]]) and STL
  ([[2026-06-22]])

## ML Parlay rules
- Max 4 legs for off card parlay
- All legs must have XGB + Consensus agreement
- Nuclear juice (-180+) dilutes parlay value — only include if other legs provide plus money
- Keep stake small — $5-15 max on parlays

## Recovery parlay rule
- Only fires after 2+ red days
- Max 1 unit stake
- 3 correlated legs only
- Track in chat not card

## SGP cashout rule — CRITICAL
When 2 of 3 SGP legs lock early → cash out immediately. Don't wait for the 3rd leg.
- STL SGP had YRFI ✅ + Burleson hit ✅ locked in 1st inning, cashout showed $33, walked away
  with nothing ([[2026-06-19]])

## K prop pitcher threshold
- K9 7.0+ = viable K prop candidate
- K9 below 7.0 = avoid K props regardless of model rate
- McGreevy K9 5.88 = should have been caught in research ([[2026-06-19]])

## No SB (stolen base) props — ever
SB props are too unpredictable — situational, manager dependent, score dependent.
PCA steals bases constantly in real life but the ONE day it was bet, he hit a HR instead.
See [[2026-06-20]].
